## Context: why now

- Enterprise buyers consolidated onto fewer vendors through 2026
- Procurement cycles stretched from 60 to 110 days
- Security review, not pricing, is now the gating step
- Quote from Dana Whitfield, VP Infrastructure at Northwind Logistics:
  "Procurement stopped asking what it costs and started asking who else runs it."

### Where we landed in FY26

- Revenue growth 34% (vs 21% prior year)
- Monthly active seats 1.9M (up from 1.4M)
- Net revenue retention 112% (target was 108%)

## The problem

- Three teams each bought overlapping tooling independently
- Combined annual spend: $4.2M
- No shared policy layer, so every compliance ask is bespoke work

## What we propose

Four investment areas:

- Reliability — cut p99 latency, eliminate top three incident classes
- Onboarding — self-serve setup under ten minutes, no solutions engineer
- Compliance — SOC 2 Type II and EU data residency
- Insight — usage analytics customers can act on unaided

### Build versus buy

- Build: ingest and transform pipeline, policy engine
- Buy: identity and SSO, billing and invoicing

## Rollout

- Q1 2027 — internal dogfood, two teams, feature-flagged, no external traffic
- Q2 2027 — design partners, six accounts under NDA, weekly feedback
- Q3 2027 — general availability in US and EU
- Q4 2027 — deprecate the three legacy tool contracts

### Migrate now or wait a quarter

- Migrate now: unblocks EU launch in Q2, one freeze window instead of two
- Wait: needs a second freeze in Q3, EU launch slips a full quarter
