Why now

  • Procurement cycles stretched from 60 to 110 days

Part 03

The proposal

One platform, four investment areas, funded for a year.

Four investment areas

⚙

Reliability

Cut p99 latency and clear the top three incident classes.

⚡

Onboarding

Self-serve setup under ten minutes, no solutions engineer.

🔒

Compliance

SOC 2 Type II and EU data residency.

📊

Insight

Usage analytics customers can act on unaided.

Compliance is highlighted because it is the one with an external deadline and the one that unblocks the EU pipeline. The other three are important but schedulable. If we can only fund two, fund compliance and reliability.

Build versus buy

What we build

  • Ingest and transform pipeline
  • Policy engine

What we buy

  • Identity and SSO
  • Billing and invoicing
The test is whether a customer would ever ask us about it in a deal. They ask about our pipeline and our policy model; they never ask whose SSO we use. Build what differentiates, buy what is table stakes.

Part 04

Rollout

Four quarters, gated on real usage

Q1 2027

Internal dogfood

Two teams, feature-flagged, no external traffic.

Q2 2027

Design partners

Six accounts under NDA with weekly feedback calls.

Q3 2027

General availability

Open in both US and EU regions.

Q4 2027

Legacy sunset

Deprecate the three overlapping tool contracts.

Each gate is a real decision point, not a status update. If design partners are not renewing by end of Q2, we do not open GA in Q3. The Q4 sunset is where the $4.2M starts coming back.

Migrate now or wait a quarter

✓ Migrate now

  • Unblocks the EU launch in Q2
  • One freeze window instead of two

✗ Wait a quarter

  • Needs a second freeze in Q3
  • EU launch slips a full quarter
The freeze window is the real argument. Every freeze costs us roughly three weeks of shipping across all teams. Doing it once is the cheaper path even though it feels more aggressive.
